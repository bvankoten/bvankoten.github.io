Research Interests

  • Applied probability and numerical analysis
  • Computer simulation of molecular systems and applications in chemistry and materials science
  • Sampling and analysis of rare events in physical systems
  • Variance reduction for Markov chain Monte Carlo methods and applications to Bayesian statistics and computational statistical physics
  • Scientific machine learning, including the development of efficient sampling methods based on neural surrogate models
  • Multigrid methods and analogous sampling methods based on coarse-grained or multiscale surrogate models
  • Numerical methods for finding saddle points and applications to the computation of reaction rates
  • Multiscale models of defects in crystalline materials, including atomistic/continuum couplings
